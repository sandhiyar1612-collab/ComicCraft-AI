## Key Takeaways from Ideation
- **Target Audience**: Writers, educators, game designers, students, and comic enthusiasts lacking professional illustration expertise.
- **Differentiator**: Unlike single-image AI generators, ComicCraft produces **coherent sequential narratives** (5 structured panels with character consistency, environment pacing, dialogues, atmospheric captions, and publication-ready multi-page PDF compilation).
- **Resilience First**: Designed with a multi-tiered fallback architecture ensuring the app never fails even if third-party AI APIs hit rate limits or downtime.
