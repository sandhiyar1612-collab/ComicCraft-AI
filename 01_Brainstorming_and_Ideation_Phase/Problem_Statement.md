# 📋 Problem Statement

## 1. Context and Background
Comics, graphic novels, and visual storyboards are among the most engaging storytelling media in human culture. However, producing a visual comic strip requires a rare combination of specialized skills:
1. **Screenwriting and Pacing**: Structuring a plot across distinct panels with narrative momentum, cliffhangers, and comedic or dramatic timing.
2. **Visual Artistry and Illustration**: Mastery of perspective, figure anatomy, character consistency, dynamic framing, ink line-weights, and coloring.
3. **Dialogue Crafting and Lettering**: Composing concise speech bubbles, environmental captions, and evocative onomatopoeic sound effects (e.g., *POW!*, *CRACK!*, *WHOOSH!*).
4. **Publishing and Layout Composition**: Assembling disparate text and graphic elements into balanced print-ready layouts adhering to publishing standards.

## 2. Core Problem
For independent storytellers, educators, students, creative hobbyists, and game designers, these requirements present an insurmountable barrier to entry:
- **Prohibitive Time and Cost**: Commissioning human illustrators costs hundreds of dollars per page and takes weeks of turn-around time.
- **Fragmented AI Tooling**: While Generative AI tools (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E, standalone LLMs) exist, they operate in silos. An author must separately write text in an LLM, manually prompt an image generator repeatedly, cope with severe character and style drift between panels, and manually stitch elements together in Photoshop or InDesign.
- **Fragile Cloud Dependencies**: Most online AI creative tools crash or return blank errors when API quotas are exhausted, leaving users with broken experiences.
- **Lack of Publication-Ready Output**: Existing generative image tools output loose PNG or JPEG files rather than cohesive, paginated, print-ready documents with comic typography and borders.

## 3. The Need for ComicCraft
There is a clear market and user need for a unified, accessible, zero-friction platform that can take a simple creative spark (a scenario, protagonist name, setting, tone, and style) and automatically orchestrate the complete story pipeline:
- Generate a logically paced 5-panel story breakdown.
- Draft contextual narration and character dialogue.
- Generate stylized comic illustrations per panel.
- Bind text, dialogue, and art into a classic Ben-Day comic book aesthetic.
- Export the complete multi-page comic book as a ready-to-share PDF.
