# 📖 User Guide: Creating Comics with ComicCraft

Welcome to **ComicCraft**! This guide walks you through every step of transforming your creative story ideas into multi-panel comic strips with publication-ready PDFs.

---

## 1. Accessing the Application
Once the local server is running, open your web browser and navigate to:
```
http://127.0.0.1:8000
```
You will be greeted by the authentic **ComicCraft** vintage comic paper studio.

---

## 2. Setting Up Your Google Gemini API Key
1. Locate the **Google Gemini API Key** bar at the top of the creation form.
2. If you already have a Gemini API key:
   - Paste your key into the password field.
   - Click the green **Save Key** button.
   - The badge will update to green: `CONNECTED`.
3. If you do not have an API key:
   - Click the link **Get Key at Google AI Studio &rarr;** ([https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)) to generate a free key.
   - *Note*: Even without an API key, ComicCraft will happily create your comic using its built-in offline procedural comic storytelling and inking engines!

---

## 3. Creating Your First Comic

### Option A: Using 1-Click Creative Presets (Fastest)
At the top of the form, click any of the vintage preset pills:
- **Cyberpunk Detective**: Takes you to neo-noir rainy Tokyo with detective Jax Mercer.
- **Enchanted Forest**: Embarks on a woodland quest with Rusty the Fox.
- **Deep Space Odyssey**: Explores distant obsidian moons with Captain Vance.
- **Superhero Awakening**: Follows vigilante Alex Shadow confronting chaos in Metro City.

### Option B: Crafting a Custom Story
Fill out the 5 creative parameters:
1. **Story Idea**: Describe the central event, challenge, or scenario (e.g., *"An adventurous archaeologist deciphers a glowing map inside an ancient pyramid"*).
2. **Main Character Name**: Name your protagonist (e.g., *"Professor Clara Vance"*).
3. **Setting / Location**: The physical environment (e.g., *"Subterranean crystal catacombs"*).
4. **Tone**: The mood of your narrative (e.g., *"Dramatic"*, *"Comedic"*, *"Suspenseful"*, *"Whimsical"*).
5. **Art Style**: Visual styling (e.g., *"Vintage Comic Book"*, *"Graphic Novel Noir"*, *"Cyberpunk Anime"*).

---

## 4. The Generation Experience
1. Click the large action button: **💥 CREATE COMIC BOOK 💥**.
2. An animated **Comic Flipbook Modal** will appear on screen.
3. You can watch the real-time progress as the system:
   - Brainstorms the 5-panel storyboard with Gemini Flash.
   - Scripts character dialogues and atmospheric sound effects with Gemini Pro.
   - Illustrates each panel scene with the comic diffusion/halftone engine.
   - Compiles the final publication-ready PDF.

---

## 5. Reading Your Comic & Exporting to PDF
Once generation completes, the **Sequential Comic Reader** will load:
- Scroll through **Panel 1 through Panel 5**.
- Each panel features full-width comic artwork, issue title banners, atmospheric scene descriptions, character dialogue bubbles, and action starbursts (*POW!*, *BAM!*).
- Click the yellow **📥 DOWNLOAD PRINTABLE PDF** button at the top or bottom of the page to save your high-resolution A4 comic book.

---

## 6. Browsing the Comic Vault (`/all-users`)
- Click **Comic Vault** in the top navigation bar or navigate to [http://127.0.0.1:8000/all-users](http://127.0.0.1:8000/all-users).
- View all previously generated comic issues.
- Inspect creation dates, file sizes, and download past issues directly.
